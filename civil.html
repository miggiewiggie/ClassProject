About the Major

The Civil Engineering program at the University of Central Florida (UCF) provides students with a strong foundation in designing, building, and maintaining infrastructure that shapes modern society. From roads and bridges to water systems and sustainable cities, civil engineers play a vital role in creating safe and efficient environments.

At UCF, students gain hands-on experience through state-of-the-art laboratories, real-world projects, and internship opportunities with industry-leading companies. The program covers key areas such as structural engineering, transportation, environmental sustainability, geotechnical engineering, and construction management.

With access to cutting-edge research, experienced faculty, and a location in one of the fastest-growing metropolitan areas, UCF's Civil Engineering program prepares graduates to meet the challenges of tomorrow’s infrastructure needs. Whether you're interested in designing resilient buildings, improving transportation networks, or developing sustainable solutions, UCF offers the knowledge and resources to help you succeed.

Major-Specific Classes

Class 1: Geotechnical Engineering

This course covers the principles of soil mechanics and foundation engineering. Students learn about soil properties, testing methods, and the design of foundations and retaining structures.

Class 2: Fluid Mechanics

This course introduces the fundamentals of fluid behavior and fluid flow. Topics include fluid properties, fluid statics, and fluid dynamics, with applications to civil engineering systems.

Class 3: Structural Analysis

This course focuses on the analysis of structures under various loads. Students learn methods for analyzing beams, trusses, and frames, and develop an understanding of structural behavior and design.
