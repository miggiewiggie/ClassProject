About the Major

The Information Technology (IT) program at UCF focuses on applying computing technologies to solve real-world problems. Students gain skills in networking, cybersecurity, software development, and IT project management, preparing them for careers in tech-driven industries.

Through hands-on labs, industry partnerships, and student organizations like Hack@UCF, students gain practical experience in cloud computing, database management, and system administration. The program emphasizes problem-solving, teamwork, and critical thinking, essential for success in IT.

Graduates secure roles in cybersecurity, IT consulting, and software development at top companies and government agencies. With access to internships, faculty research, and career resources, UCF’s IT program equips students with the skills needed for the ever-evolving tech industry.
